# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in eRecall a respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while still being clear about risks and tradeoffs.
- Welcome newcomers and help them understand project context.
- Keep technical disagreement focused on the work.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing private information without permission.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Dismissing security, privacy, or safety concerns without review.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior undermines a healthy project environment.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns, contact the maintainers through the repository owner profile or another published maintainer contact. Security issues should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
